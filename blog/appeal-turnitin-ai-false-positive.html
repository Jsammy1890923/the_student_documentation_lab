Frequently Asked Questions

Can a professor fail you based only on a Turnitin AI score?

In most accredited universities, an automated software score cannot serve as sole proof of misconduct. Institutions must establish a preponderance of evidence (51% likelihood) through corroborating proof, such as missing draft histories or severe stylometric discrepancies.

What is the false-positive rate for Turnitin AI detection?

Turnitin officially admits a 4% sentence-level false-positive rate. Independent peer-reviewed studies show false-positive rates on non-native English and structured human writing exceeding 60%, prompting universities like Vanderbilt and MIT to disable the feature entirely.

How do I prove I wrote an essay if I am falsely accused of using AI?
