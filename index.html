Dual-Zone Contrast: The Triage Protocol

Your survival in an academic hearing depends strictly on defense posture. Avoid Red Zone traps; build the Blue Zone firewall immediately.

Standard: Preponderance of Evidence (51%)

The Red Zone: The 48-Hour Trap

  • Emotional Apologies: Sending "I'm sorry" or "I'll do better" emails to faculty, which conduct boards record as formal confessions of guilt.
  • Confessing Under Pressure: Speculating on causes during high-stress unrecorded meetings (e.g., "Maybe it was Grammarly?").
  • Deleting Evidence: Deleting messy rough drafts, outline files, or browser search histories that actually demonstrate human thought.
  • Oral Debates: Attempting to debate detector accuracy without raw, unredacted tool reports in hand.

The Blue Zone: The Defense Shield

  • Cease Communication & Editing (Rule #1): Immediate cessation of informal emails and zero further edits to the flagged file.
  • Unalterable Revision History: Exporting Google Docs version history and OneDrive track changes proving character-by-character labor.
  • Browser Cache & Telemetry Freeze: Archiving research PDFs, library timestamps, and Canvas LMS activity logs into an immutable state.
  • Cryptographic Hash Integrity: Generating SHA-256 verification hashes for all files before transmitting them to hearing panels.
